Why we built this

It started with one question at home.

WalletWise started at home. I run the household money, and my wife was always asking the same question: “which card should I use for this?”

So we built a simple guide: one tap, the right card. The guessing stopped, and the earning added up — roughly $1,000–$2,500 back a year in our own wallet, without spending a single dollar more. Just our regular expenses as usual: from groceries, to dining out, to trips — or even a bubble tea!

Around then, we also got curious about credit. Ours kept fluctuating, and we realized how little anyone actually teaches you about it — yet it quietly shapes everything: your everyday purchases, the interest rate on a car loan, even whether you get approved at all. Financial literacy just isn't something most of us are ever handed.

That's the real point of WalletWise: helping people see their own situation clearly — the advantages worth grabbing, and the things worth watching for. Use what you already have, wisely. Wallet-wise.

Good questions

The things people ask first.

Is my card information safe?

Yes. We only use the last 3 digits and a nickname to draw your cards — never full numbers. Or none at all — it’s your card, so name it whatever you like, or nothing. Nothing you type into the app is saved or sent anywhere; it runs entirely on your phone.

Do I have to spend more to benefit?

No — that's the whole point. WalletWise is built around the spending you already do. You just route each purchase to the card that pays you back the most.

What happens when my cards change?

Tell us and we'll update your app. Card reward rules shift over time too, so we refresh everything on a quarterly schedule at no extra charge.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. It runs in any phone browser and installs to the home screen on both iPhone and Android — the steps differ slightly, and we'll walk you through it.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's a convenience tool for using the cards you already own. We don't sell cards and we don't earn commissions — the recommendation is simply the best match for your wallet.
